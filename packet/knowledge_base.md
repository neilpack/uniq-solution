## 1. Custom printed paper ice cream cups

- Print: full color included (gradients and photos are fine)
- Color: **close match only. No Pantone (PMS) guarantee.**
- Setup fee: **none**
- Lead time: **7 to 10 business days** after the customer approves the proof

### Custom order rules
- **Shipping:** free freight in the **lower 48 US states** when the order subtotal (cups + setup) is **$2,500 or more**. Under $2,500: **$95 flat**.
- **Outside the lower 48** (Alaska, Hawaii, Canada, Puerto Rico, anywhere international): we have no standard rate. **Escalate to the owner.**
- **Artwork:** customer sends a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF, or SVG). We lay out simple logos for free. Custom design work is $75/hour.
- **Proofs:** every custom order gets a digital proof. Lead times start when the customer approves the proof, not when they first email.
- **Payment:** **50% deposit** to start production. The balance is due before shipping.
- **Quotes** are estimates, valid for 30 days, and do not include sales tax.
- Custom lids are not offered. Stock lids are sold separately (see catalog).

## 3. Product facts
- **Paper ice cream cups** are food-safe, freezer-safe, and rated for **cold and frozen foods only**. Not for hot food. Not microwave-safe.
- **Compostable (PLA-lined) cups and wooden spoons** are **commercially compostable only. They are not home compostable.** PLA cups are rated up to **110°F (43°C)**. Not for hot soup or hot drinks.
- **Kraft hot cups** are rated for hot drinks up to 190°F.
- We sell packaging only. We don't sell food, ice cream, or machines.

## 4. Returns, damage, and defects
- **Stock items:** returnable if **unopened** and within **30 days of delivery**. 15% restocking fee. The customer pays return shipping unless we made the mistake. **Opened cases cannot be returned.**
- **Shipping damage:** report within **7 days of delivery** with photos. We send free replacements for the damaged cases. No need to return them.
- **Custom printed items** cannot be returned. **Manufacturing defects** (misprints, wrong size, wrong quantity) must be reported within **14 days of delivery** with photos. We will reprint or credit.
- **Color variation on in-house digital orders** within the "close match" standard is **not a defect**. Customers approve a proof that states this.

## 5. Payment terms, discounts, price matching
- We take credit card and ACH.
- **Net 30 terms** are only for accounts with **6+ months of order history** and the owner's approval.
- **Staff may not offer discounts or price matches.** Published pricing is the only pricing. Any discount or price-match request goes to the owner.

## 6. When a human (the owner) must handle it
Flag `needs_human = Yes` when any of these are true:
1. The estimated order value is **$5,000 or more**.
2. A **rush**, or a guaranteed delivery date that is tighter than our standard lead times.
3. **Discount, price-match, or payment-terms** requests.
4. Shipping **outside the lower 48**.
5. A **complaint about a custom order**, or an upset customer asking for something outside policy.
6. Legal threats, chargebacks, press, or anything that looks **suspicious or like a security risk**.
7. A question this knowledge base **does not answer**. **Never guess.**

An AI draft is still useful for flagged emails. The owner reviews it before anything is sent.
